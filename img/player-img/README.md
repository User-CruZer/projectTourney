# Player pictures (VS intro)

Put one picture per player here. It shows big on that player's side when the "VS" intro plays on the draft overlays
(a match loaded from the bracket, or player names saved).

- **File name** = the player's name in lower case, spaces and symbols turned into dashes:
  `MOCHIEL` → `mochiel.png`, `RYUU ZUKI` → `ryuu-zuki.png`, `QING GIAO` → `qing-giao.webp`, `WILD CARD` → `wild-card.png`.
- **Format:** `.webp`, `.png` or `.jpg` (tried in that order).
- **Shape:** portrait, about 3 : 4 (shown at 440 × 560, cropped from the top). Transparent PNGs look best.
- A player without a picture just shows their name.
- After adding pictures, commit and push like any other change.
